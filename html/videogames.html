Minecraft (3000+ Hours)

Minecraft is possibly the most popular video game of all time, known for its open-ended nature, with many possibilities ranging from survival, to building, to redstone, to coding, to PvE, to PvP...
The possibilities are endless.

I don't exactly remember my first exposure to Minecraft. Perhaps it was when I saw a friend play the Beta release on their ipad, or when the owners of the house my family was staying over at for vacation let me on their Xbox 360 and let me loose. My early Minecraft days were moments stolen away on bootleg editions, hidden in innocuous folders on cracked iPads and phones, convincing people to let me play on their computers, the like. It was only in 2019 that I managed to buy my own Java account, and the rest is history.

Terraria (1500+ Hours)

Terraria is one of the best action RPG games out there. With a sandbox core, an absurd amount of content, and an addicting progression loop, it is one of the best two-dimensional pixel games out there. Like with Minecraft, I've especially enjoyed the time spent with friends beating the game or just hanging out.

Terraria was the first game I bought on Steam and it has remained arguably my favorite since. One of the aspects I enjoy the most is the feeling of weaving in between death during bossfights - I'm very much a bossfight-oriented Terraria player, although it has much more to offer than that. I've especially enjoyed the time spent beating the game or just hanging out with friends, and marvel at all the great mods the community has made for the love of this game.

Factorio (100+ Hours)

Build and expand a factory...forever. Conveyor belts. Assemblers. Chemical Plants. Fend off foes that balk at your increasing cloud of pollution. Use the resources of the planet Nauvis to build a rocket and escape.

Factorio is one of the newer additions to my game library, but I already know that it's going to be a staple. Surprisingly, I've learned a lot about Computer Science and a bit about Information Technology from the game, learning up how to set up logic gates, optimizing automation, etc.
